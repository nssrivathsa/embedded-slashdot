EmbeddedDot 7-Source Curation Architecture & 3-Year Policy

How Dynamic 3-Year Lookback Works Across 7 Free Sources

You can browse any day from today back 1,095 days (3 years) without pre-fetching gigabytes of archives. Whenever you navigate to a date (or click Load & Append Previous Day), EmbeddedDot queries 7 distinct date-windowed channels concurrently:

  1. CNX Software (Embedded Systems News): Queries cnx-software.com/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?after=...&before=... for exact historical coverage of Edge AI SoCs, NPUs, RISC-V boards, and MCUs.
  2. Free Embedded & AI Newsletters: Matches historical editions from Interrupt by Memfault (Atom archive), SemiAnalysis, Import AI, Ahead of AI, and Last Week in AI (Substack archives).
  3. Hackaday Hardware Dispatch: Queries hackaday.com/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?after=...&before=... for FPGA, microcontroller, and bare-metal AI builds.
  4. OpenAlex Peer-Reviewed IEEE / ACM Papers: Queries api.openalex.org/works by publication date across IEEE TCAD, IEEE Micro, ACM TECS, and IEEE IoT Journal.
  5. Hugging Face Daily Papers: Queries huggingface.co/api/daily_papers?date=YYYY-MM-DD for community-curated quantization and on-device SLM papers.
  6. arXiv Preprints: Queries export.arxiv.org/api/query with a submittedDate window across cs.AR, cs.LG, cs.CV, eess.SP, and cs.PF.
  7. Hacker News Algolia Timestamp Search: Queries hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search_by_date with exact Unix timestamps for community hardware & SDK releases.
